How to play

🫧 Pimples

  • Lean in: tap a spot on her face to get close. Step back to see the whole face again.
  • Pinch & squeeze: two fingers either side of a pimple, then squeeze them together until it pops. One finger only shows you where to pinch.
  • Keep holding to drain it, and let go the moment it's empty for a Perfect. The core comes out last, then clear fluid: that's your cue. Squeeze on and it bleeds.
  • The right tool jumps into your hand: touch an ingrown hair and you get the Tweezers; touch a pimple and you get your fingers back.
  • Loop for blackheads. Needle first on deep cysts and hard little milia. Tweezers for hairs: grab and pull. Wipe to clean up.
  • Cysts and the Big One leave a sac behind once drained: grab it with the Tweezers and pull it out slowly.
  • Skin type is on every patient card. Oily is juicier, dry needs a longer squeeze, and sensitive skin skips the steam.
  • Aftercare: Spray disinfects a popped spot; Patch covers it. Both score extra, more if you spray first.

🧴 Full treatment

  • Five steps: lather and rinse, steam, pop, wipe, then soothing gel. Every other patient ends with a charcoal mask: spread it, fan it dry, peel it off.
  • Supplies: tap a closed bottle, pack or jar and open it by hand: twist the pump, peel the seal, lift the lid, pull the cap. Used wipes go in the bin.
  • Masks and gel stay off the eyes, brows and lips. Glowing rings show any spot you missed.
  • You can switch the extra steps off in Settings.

👂 Ear Wax

  • Every ear is a different person with a different problem. Scoop: hold a lump until it loosens, then drag it out. Drops soften rock-hard wax. Tweezers grab stuck objects and bugs. Flush washes out crumbs. Mind the eardrum.

👃 Pore Strip

  • Wet the nose, drag a strip on, smooth it down, blow it dry, then grab the bottom edge and peel up, slowly. Too fast hurts and leaves plugs behind.

🤨 Brows

  • Grab each stray hair and pull it out, then swipe the pencil along each brow. Don't touch the brows themselves while plucking.

💄 Makeup & 💇‍♀️ Hair Salon

  • Makeup: pick a product and a shade and swipe it on. She stars the shades she wants; match them for a bigger tip.
  • Hair Salon: shampoo, rinse, trim, colour and blow-dry. She tells you what she wants. Upgrade your stations to offer more and earn more.

⭐ VIP visits, stars and coins

  • VIP: one client, four treatments in a row, then her review out of five and a tip. Today's VIP tips double.
  • Every patient has 3 stars: one for clearing the face, two for their own goals. Coins buy upgrades and pus styles in the Shop.
  • Awards pay coins and raise your rank. Reviews are kept in your guest book on the Awards screen.
