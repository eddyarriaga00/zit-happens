How to pop

  1. Pinch & squeeze: put two fingers either side of a pimple and squeeze them together to build pressure until it pops. One finger only shows you where to pinch.
  2. Keep holding to drain it. Watch the ring empty out.
  3. Let go right when it's empty for a Perfect. Squeeze too long and it bleeds.
  4. Supplies: everything on the tray works. Tap a closed bottle, pack or jar and open it by hand: lift the lid, peel the seal, twist the pump, pull the cap. Pump the cleanser and gel onto the face. Once a pack is open, just wipe: a fresh wipe or pad comes out by itself, and a dirty one flicks into the bin.
  5. Stray hairs: from level 4, loose hairs show up around the brows, lip and chin. Pluck them with the Tweezers.
  6. Peel-off mask: every other patient finishes with a charcoal mask: scoop it on, spread it, fan it dry with your finger, then peel it off.
  7. Aftercare: Spray disinfects popped spots and calms the redness. Patch sticks a little heart, star or dot on a popped spot. Both give bonus points, more if you spray first.
  8. Cysts are big and stubborn. Blackheads are quick. Golden ones are lucky.
  9. Milia: tiny hard white pearls (from level 6). They won't squeeze until you nick them with the Needle.
  10. When to let go: the core pops out last, then clear fluid wells up. That's your cue. Keep squeezing and it bleeds.
  11. Makeup: a quick side mode. Swipe lipstick, blush and eyeshadow onto the right spots.
  12. Hair Salon: your own salon. Each client gets a shampoo, rinse, fringe trim and blow-dry. Buy the Colour Bar to dye hair and the Styling Station to restyle it. She tells you what she wants, and it's starred in the choices: match it for a bigger tip. Upgrade stations and decor with coins to offer more and earn more.
  13. Awards: 30 awards for milestones, each paying coins and raising your rank.
  14. Rewards: every patient and every tool is open from the start. Clearing a patient for the first time still pays a bonus: a free upgrade, a pus style or coins.
  15. Stars & coins: every patient has 3 stars: one for clearing the face, two for their own goals. Coins buy upgrades and pus styles in the Shop.
  16. Full treatment: each patient gets 5 steps: lather and rinse the face, steam it to open the pores, pop, wipe up, then spread soothing gel. You can switch the extra steps off in Settings.
  17. Tools: the Loop is for blackheads: press one and it pops in a flash. The Needle opens deep cysts so they squeeze fast. Tweezers pull out ingrown hairs: grab and drag. The Wipe pad cleans big areas.
  18. Ear Wax: hold a chunk (wiggle it!) until it loosens, then drag it out of the ear. Never poke the eardrum.
