How to pop

  1. Press & hold a pimple to build pressure until it pops. Even better: pinch it between two fingers and squeeze them together.
  2. Keep holding to drain it. Watch the ring empty out.
  3. Let go right when it's empty for a Perfect. Squeeze too long and it bleeds.
  4. Swipe anywhere else to wipe up the mess.
